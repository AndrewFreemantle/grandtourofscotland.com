FAQ

The answers below come from the current event information. A fuller FAQ will be published here and is likely to be updated several times before the event.

Is it a race?

No. It is an endurance challenge with a time limit, run under Audax UK and LRM rules. There are also some additional specific event rules and conditions.

How long do I have to complete it?

Just over 200 hours, subject to the final route. The minimum speed limit is 10km/h and that includes all time off the bike – stopping, eating or resting.

Are there qualifying rides?

There are no pre-qualifying or qualifying requirements.

Will there be food at the controls?

There will be some limited food options at event controls. Some controls have no event facilities at all, and much of the route is remote: outside of 9–5 hours you will struggle to find anything commercially. The control list shows which controls are planned to have food.

Can I sleep at controls?

Yes, at a number of event controls. Because of the remote and exposed nature of the route there will be controls where riders can rest and sleep – see the beds column in the control list.

Is there a bag drop?

There will be a limited bag drop service to some of the controls. The controls that are planned to have bag drop are marked in the control list. Full details will be confirmed in the final event information.

Do I need a tracker?

It is planned that trackers will be mandatory for all riders, and they will be included in your entry fee. This is primarily for safety, and also to help us manage the flow of riders and demand on controls. See live tracking.

Will E-Brevet be available?

It is planned that E-Brevet will be available as an option, but you will still need to visit each of the controls in order.

What happens if I get ahead of the control opening times?

Then you’re on your own, and you have two choices: fend for yourself, or slow down and wait until the controls open. You can still complete the route as long as you finish in time.

What about the ferries?

There are two crossings. The first is Gourock to Dunoon (Hunters Quay) with Western Ferries at 205km, expected to start from about 6.30am. The second is Corran at 510km; the event is planned so that riders will not cross there until the Wednesday morning. If you get there and cross on the Tuesday night then you’re on your own.

What sort of bike do I need?

The majority of the route is on good tarmac roads, with two gravel sections through the forest between Fort William and Invergarry – about 15km in total – which are rideable on a road bike as long as you’re sensible. Make sure your bike is in very good condition and ready for 2000km: there are very few bike shops or repair facilities along the route.

Can I book my own accommodation?

Yes. You are very welcome to book hotels, hostels or B&Bs along the route and eat at cafes, shops or other places. Be aware that facilities are very limited in remote areas, and commercial options are likely to be very busy and often relatively expensive.

How do I enter?

Entries will be through the Audax UK website and you will need to be a registered user of that site. See enter for the detail.

Can’t find your answer?

Email event@grandtourofscotland.com and we’ll add common questions to this page.

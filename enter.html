Enter

Entries will be through the Audax UK website. You will need to be a registered user of the site – registration is free.

We would suggest you register in advance of trying to enter. If you are not a paid member of Audax UK you can still enter, but will have to pay a £3 temporary membership fee.

More information on how to enter will follow nearer the time. There will not be any pre-qualifying or qualifying requirements.

  • 1. Register

    Create a free account on the Audax UK website, well before entries open.

  • 2. Watch for entries

    Entry details and the opening date will be announced here nearer the time.

  • 3. Enter

    Enter through Audax UK. Non-members pay a £3 temporary membership fee.

Before you enter

This is a 2000km endurance challenge through very remote areas with a minimum speed of 10km/h including all time off the bike. Please read about the event and the control opening times so you know what you are entering.
